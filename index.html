Clean the lint, or replace the line?

A Dallas dryer that needs two cycles is usually a clogged exhaust — or a foil hose that should not be the exhaust. Those are different jobs. This page is the split, plus phones copied from each shop’s own site.

First metro: Dallas–Fort Worth. Texas law governs this page. A homeowner on r/Dallas reported a $450 quote for a two-story clean. Other local comments described about $200 and $250. Those are homeowner reports, not a rate card.

Ask before the truck rolls

  • Is this a brush-and-vacuum of the existing run, or a new metal line?
  • Will they open the exterior hood and show the lint?
  • Is the short hose behind the dryer vinyl or foil? Those are not a code exhaust duct.
  • Does the price change if the vent exits the roof?

Not a contractor, not a city permit ruling, and not a quote. Confirm the current Dallas dryer-exhaust rules with the city before a reroute.
