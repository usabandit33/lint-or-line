Answers a Dallas homeowner can say out loud

Is the clean the same as a new line?

No. Brushing lint out of the pipe that is already there does not replace a vinyl or foil hose, and it does not shorten a run that is too long. Ask for the job by name.

What should the transition behind the dryer be?

A short metal transition, not a plastic vinyl hose and not foil used as the exhaust duct. Shops on this list say they flag that material when they see it. Get that in the quote if you want it swapped the same visit.

Why do roof vents cost more?

The tech has to reach the termination. A side-wall hood and a roof cap are not the same access. Ask before you book.

What did Dallas homeowners say they were quoted?

On r/Dallas, one poster said they were quoted $450 for a two-story home. Comments mentioned about $200 for a second-floor roof exit and $250 for a two-story clean. Treat those as reports, not a price list. Source: r/Dallas dryer-vent thread.

How to book

  1. Look at the hose behind the dryer. Note vinyl, foil, or metal.
  2. Find the outdoor flap. Side wall or roof.
  3. Call a number on the Dallas page and ask clean versus line.
  4. Ask what they will show you when they finish (lint pulled, hood open).
